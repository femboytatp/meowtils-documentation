# **PartyDetector**

#### Detects if a party joins your lobby by checking how many players joins your lobby within a short amount of time.

---

## Scale

Changes the size of HUD text.

## Ping sound

Plays a sound for alerts.

## Alert missed

Counts and alerts how many players that were missed/couldn't be counted. This would include all players that joined before you did.

## Show HUD

Displays missed players & missed parties on your HUD.

!!! Note

    This cannot be 100% accurate as missed parties are only estimated depending on players that joined before you, and the max size of teams in your currently selected Bedwars mode.

## Bedwars 2s

Enable counting in Bedwars doubles, 2 players per party,

## Bedwars 3s

Enable counting in Bedwars threes, 3 players per party.

## Bedwars 4s

Enable counting in Bedwars fours, 4 players per party.

## Bedwars 4v4

Enable counting in the 4v4 Bedwars mode, this is also 4 players per party.
